At first glance, a diamond may stand out for its brilliance. However, when examined through a specialized viewer, it can reveal something even more impressive: the pattern known as Hearts and Arrows.

02

A hidden geometry

Eight arrows above.
Eight hearts below.

This effect appears in round diamonds cut with an exceptional level of precision and symmetry. When viewed from the top, the facets create a pattern resembling eight arrows. From the bottom, heart-shaped figures can be seen.

What creates the pattern

Not colour.
Not clarity.
Precision.

The Hearts and Arrows effect does not depend only on a diamond’s clarity or color. It is primarily the result of a precise cut, balanced proportions, and excellent optical symmetry.

The result

When these elements work together, light reflects more evenly, producing greater brilliance, contrast, and sparkle.
